About

I make graphics that have to hold up on air: fast to produce, clear at a glance, consistent every day.

I work as a Computer Graphics Artist at Astro AWANI, where the work runs from on-air packaging to motion infographics and social cutdowns. Before that I spent time in LED and large format visuals, and started out as an intern in multimedia production.

I am currently reading for a Master of Science in Information Technology at FTMK, Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka. My first degree was in Computer Science with a specialisation in Interactive Media, which is where the mix of design, motion and code comes from.
